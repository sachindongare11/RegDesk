ARTICLE General-Principles-1: Acting honestly, fairly and professionally
A regulated entity must act honestly, fairly and professionally in the best
interests of its customers and the integrity of the market. A regulated entity
must ensure that in all its dealings with customers it acts with due skill,
care and diligence in the best interests of its customers.

ARTICLE General-Principles-3: Avoiding conflicts of interest
A regulated entity must seek to avoid conflicts of interest, and where conflicts
cannot be avoided, must ensure that customers are fairly treated through
disclosure, internal rules, or declining to act where a conflict would
otherwise prejudice the interests of a customer.

ARTICLE Chapter-3-Disclosure: Provision of information
A regulated entity must ensure that all information it provides to a consumer
is clear, accurate, up to date, and written in plain, comprehensible language.
Key information, including charges and the existence of a cooling-off period,
must be brought to the consumer's attention at the proposal or pre-contractual
stage, not buried in later documentation.

ARTICLE Chapter-10-Errors: Errors and complaints resolution
A regulated entity must have in place a written procedure for the proper
handling of complaints, and must acknowledge a complaint within 5 business
days, provide a regular update at least every 20 business days, and issue a
final response within 40 business days of the complaint being made, unless
both the regulated entity and consumer agree to an extended timeline.

ARTICLE Chapter-4-Knowing-the-Consumer: Knowing the consumer and suitability
Before offering, recommending, arranging or providing a product or service, a
regulated entity must gather sufficient information from the consumer to be
reasonably satisfied that the product or service is suitable for that consumer,
having regard to their financial needs, objectives, and capacity to absorb
loss.
